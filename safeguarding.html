Our safeguarding posture

Trust is built through clear practices.

Safeguarding means creating environments where people are treated with dignity, listened to carefully, and supported within wise boundaries. It matters especially when serving youth, newcomers, and people navigating vulnerable seasons.

PVOICE OF HOPE uses a care-focused approach that includes volunteer screening, conduct expectations, appropriate supervision, confidentiality, clear reporting, and referral when needs require additional help.

Safeguarding standards

The commitments that guide our spaces.

01

Safe welcome

We aim to create spaces where every person is treated with respect, dignity, patience, and care.

02

Volunteer screening

Volunteer roles begin with application, conversation, reference checks, and background checks where required.

03

Clear boundaries

Mentoring and youth-facing support should follow wise communication, meeting, confidentiality, and supervision boundaries.

04

Code of conduct

Volunteers and leaders are expected to serve with humility, integrity, respect, and appropriate behavior.

05

Concern reporting

Concerns should be documented, escalated to designated leadership, and handled with seriousness and care.

06

Referral support

When a need is beyond our scope, we seek to connect people to appropriate professional, pastoral, or community support.

If there is a concern

Speak up early. We take concerns seriously.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. For concerns related to a PVOICE OF HOPE program, volunteer, event, or participant experience, contact the team so the concern can be received and handled responsibly.

How concerns are handled

A simple care pathway.

Step 1

Receive

The concern is received with care and without dismissal.

Step 2

Document

Relevant details are recorded carefully and respectfully.

Step 3

Escalate

Designated leadership reviews the concern and determines the appropriate response.

Step 4

Support

People affected are supported with follow-up, referral, and next steps where appropriate.
