Trust and Transparency

How we handle personal information shared through the website, forms, events, and programs.

Trust & transparency Privacy commitment PVOICE OF HOPE

PVOICE OF HOPE respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share through this website, forms, events, and programs.

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, organization, volunteer interests, partnership details, and messages submitted through forms.

We do not sell personal information. We handle submitted information with care and use it only for communication, program coordination, and community support.
