Our mission

To make belonging
easier to find.

PVOICE OF HOPE exists so newcomers, Black youth, and young people do not have to navigate identity, loneliness, faith, leadership, or transition alone.

We create pathways for mentorship, leadership development, newcomer support, faith growth, mental wellness, and community engagement.

Program pathways

Our program pathways.

Six clear pathways connect people to mentorship, leadership, newcomer support, faith, wellness, and community.

Step 1

Youth Mentorship

Encouraging guidance and accountable relationships for the next faithful step.

Step 2

Leadership Development

Practical skills, confidence, communication, and purpose-driven leadership.

Step 3

Newcomer Support

Welcome, orientation, peer connection, and community support in a new season.

Step 4

Faith & Spiritual Growth

Prayer, discipleship, biblical encouragement, and identity rooted in Christ.

Step 5

Mental Wellness

Safe conversations that strengthen resilience, confidence, and healthy relationships.

Step 6

Community Engagement

Gatherings, outreach, volunteering, and friendships that turn belonging into impact.

Before you reach out

Quick answers before you reach out.

Know who can join, what support looks like, and how to take the first step.

Newcomers, Black youth, young adults, families, volunteers, mentors, churches, and partners who want faith-centered community, mentorship, leadership development, and belonging are welcome to connect.
