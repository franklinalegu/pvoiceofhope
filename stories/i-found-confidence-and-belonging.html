Participant Story

“I found confidence and belonging.”

A youth participant story of hope, mentorship, faith, leadership, and community.

Stories of Hope Read the journey PVOICE OF HOPE

When I first arrived in Canada, I was grateful, but beneath the gratitude I carried a quiet fear I could not explain.

I was a young Black newcomer trying to understand a new culture, a new school system, a new community, and a new version of myself.

At a PVOICE OF HOPE gathering, people did not just ask my name; they listened to my story. They made space for me to breathe, speak, and be seen.

Through mentorship, I met someone who reminded me my voice mattered. She prayed with me, encouraged me, challenged me, and helped me take faithful steps.

Today, I am still growing, but I no longer feel alone. PVOICE OF HOPE gave me mentors, friends, faith, courage, and a place to belong.
