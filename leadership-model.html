Leadership Model

How care, mentorship, and accountability are organized.

PVOICE OF HOPE is structured to serve people with clarity, wisdom, and consistency. This model explains how the mission is stewarded and how support is coordinated.

Why this model matters

People are served best when care is intentional.

Our leadership model exists so newcomers, Black youth, young people, volunteers, mentors, churches, and partners know how support is organized. It keeps the work relational while making responsibilities clear.

The model is not built around titles alone. It is built around stewardship: listening carefully, matching people with appropriate support, protecting trust, and helping people take the next faithful step.

Core responsibilities

The roles that keep the mission healthy.

01

Vision & Stewardship

Guides the mission, protects the values, and ensures that programs remain aligned with faith, purpose, and community care.

02

Programs & Mentorship

Coordinates pathways for mentorship, leadership development, newcomer support, faith growth, wellness, and belonging.

03

Volunteer & Outreach Care

Supports volunteers, event teams, community outreach, hospitality, onboarding, and follow-up.

04

Safeguarding & Boundaries

Helps ensure youth-facing and care-related activities are handled with appropriate screening, boundaries, and reporting pathways.

05

Prayer & Spiritual Support

Offers prayer, faith conversations, encouragement, and wise referral when needs require additional support.

06

Partnerships & Community

Builds relationships with churches, schools, nonprofits, businesses, mentors, and community organizations.

How support flows

From first conversation to continued care.

Step 1

Listen

We begin by understanding the person, their context, their questions, and the kind of support they are seeking.

Step 2

Discern

We identify whether mentorship, newcomer support, faith encouragement, volunteering, partnership, or referral is the right next step.

Step 3

Connect

We connect the person to the most appropriate pathway, conversation, event, mentor, or support opportunity.

Step 4

Follow through

We encourage consistency, healthy boundaries, communication, and continued community connection.

Accountability commitments

Leadership should make people safer, not smaller.

Our care model is shaped by clarity, humility, safeguarding awareness, confidentiality, collaboration, and responsible referral when a need is beyond our scope.
